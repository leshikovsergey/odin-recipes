SIMPLE CHARLOTTE WITH APPLES IN THE OVEN LUXURIOUS

Baking powder and beaten eggs are the key to incredible fluffiness. A simple charlotte with apples turns out especially airy, tender on the inside and rosy on the outside. The pieces of fruit are distributed evenly, and the sponge cake is porous and very tasty.

Ingredients

  1. Wheat flour 200 gr
  2. Sugar 200 gr
  3. Eggs 4 pieces
  4. Apples 3 pieces
  5. Baking Powder 1 teaspoon

Calories

  • Proteins 12 %
  • Fat 7 %
  • Carbohydrates 81 %
  • Total 197 kcal
