Salmon in the oven is juicy and soft

If we start talking about good fish, then salmon will be at the top of the parade. Its unique taste properties and almost complete absence of bones allow any cook in the world to delight and surprise with new dishes.

Ingredients

  1. Salmon 300 gr
  2. Soy sauce 2 tablespoons
  3. Vegetable oil 2 tablespoons
  4. Lemon 1 pieces

Calories
